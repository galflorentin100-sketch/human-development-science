# HDS — Claude Continuation Handoff

## Purpose
Continue the existing Human Development Science (HDS) project without changing its mission, scientific standards, architecture, or terminology.

HDS is a scientific organization first and a practical human-development/training organization second. The system exists to discover what reliably develops human capabilities, turn sufficiently supported knowledge into measurable training, test transfer and retention, and continuously improve itself.

Core loop:
Research → construct → operationalize → measure → evidence → hypothesis → intervention → training → experience/data → analysis → transfer → retention → replication/review → protocol → improved training.

## Absolute invariants
1. Never fabricate evidence, studies, participants, measurements, statistics, citations, sources, or missing values.
2. FACT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS and OPINION must remain distinct.
3. Provenance is not efficacy.
4. A PROPOSED claim is not a SUPPORTED claim.
5. A finding is not scientific knowledge until its evidence/review requirements are satisfied.
6. Training science itself is evidence that must be tested; do not assume a training method works because it is plausible.
7. Do not infer causality from descriptive or observational data.
8. When evidence is insufficient, preserve uncertainty and create the research needed to resolve it.
9. Contradictory evidence must trigger review, not silent deletion or downgrade.
10. Transfer and retention are required when the intended claim concerns durable human development.
11. AI may discover, synthesize, reason and propose; it may not declare scientific truth.
12. Never weaken a scientific gate just to make a test, demo, autonomous loop or UI pass.

## Autonomous continuation
When the user says continue / תמשיך, continue implementation from this roadmap without asking which small feature to choose, unless a genuinely blocking scientific or product decision is required.

Default loop:
inspect → implement → test → fix → integrate → commit → continue.

Never report tests as passing unless actually run.

## Scientific firewall
AI may propose hypotheses, evidence searches, experiments, analyses and training ideas. It may not approve evidence, declare a hypothesis a fact, silently alter scientific state, bypass permissions, bypass cost controls, bypass safety, or publish externally without authorization.

## Definition of done
A feature is done only when implementation exists, invalid paths are rejected, governance is enforced at service boundaries, API permissions are correct, important transitions are auditable, tests cover success and failure paths, adjacent lifecycle stages integrate, and no scientific claim is stronger than its evidence.

## Product identity
Do not turn HDS into a generic self-help app, motivational content system, wellness tracker, productivity SaaS, or unsupported coaching chatbot.

The eventual product is a serious human-performance and scientific training system: research-backed, measurable, adaptive, motivating and scientifically honest.

Central principle:
Improve the system without compromising the science.
